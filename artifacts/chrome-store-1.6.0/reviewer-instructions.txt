Install the package and allow the background Local Finland data tab to finish importing. Open a route owned by your reviewer account on a supported desktop Komoot planner or tour edit page. Use Analyse route: the overview and trail checks run together. Click a riding section or warning to zoom and highlight it; Highlight gaps shows missing MTB-grade stretches without moving the map. Generate the proposed POI preview, inspect suggestions with their magnifier controls, select individual new points and approve adding them. Matching existing points show Already on route and cannot be selected. Save explicitly in Komoot if you want to retain added points. Use a disposable route for insertion checks. Review settings, trail colours, the privacy policy and Local Finland data controls. Online OSM requests outside local coverage may be throttled. No private developer route or account credentials are required.
