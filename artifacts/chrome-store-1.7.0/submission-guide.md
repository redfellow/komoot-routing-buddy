1. Upload only `chrome--komoot-routing-buddy-1.7.0.zip`, generated by the validated release pipeline. Do not upload the listing-assets folder or a ZIP containing an outer directory. Verify exactly one `manifest.json`, at ZIP root, and `data/finland/snapshot.json` for local data metadata. Verify the ZIP includes all five new ride/POI modules and its manifest version is 1.7.0.
2. Replace the listing description and short description with this folder's text, and upload the three numbered screenshots. Existing image assets can remain if accurate. Onboarding is included: a three-step guide shown once, with replay in Settings.

5. Push the updated policy and verify the public URL contains the 2026-10-14 text and approved-POI paragraph before submission: https://github.com/redfellow/komoot-routing-buddy/blob/main/PRIVACY.md . Packaged privacy.html must match.
6. Give reviewers instructions: open a reviewer-owned Komoot route in the planner or edit page, wait for bundled Finland import, use Analyse route, inspect a section/warning, generate POI preview, approve selected points and save explicitly in Komoot. Matching existing POIs are disabled to prevent duplicates. Login may be needed; do not provide your private account credentials or rely on a private example route.
7. Smoke-test the built package, including existing settings after upgrade, local-data initialization, analysis, map-style changes, warning zoom, missing-grade highlighting, and POI approval on a disposable route. A source-only test is not a package test.
8. Submit for review after all fields are consistent. Prefer deferred/manual publishing to control when the approved update goes live. Upload and review submission alone do not update installed copies.

Official update guidance: https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/update
